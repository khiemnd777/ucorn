# Well Technical Research

## Workflow

Read [source register](../../../docs/well-schematic/sources.md) and relevant [catalog](../../../docs/well-schematic/component-catalog.md). Browse current primary sources: SLB Energy Glossary for definitions, official manufacturer documents for model-specific assemblies, and actual standards text only when accessible and relevant. Existing candidate URL is not verified evidence.

For each claim record URL/title, organization, checked date, model/edition if applicable, scope and status. Distinguish definition-backed function, family-only variant assumption and proposed UCORN convention. A source for casing is not proof of centralizer dimensions or liner connector compatibility. Do not invent standard number/clause/symbol/rating or cite search snippets as specification.

When retrieval fails, mark unverified/unavailable, preserve the narrow unresolved claim and try a relevant official alternative. Do not bypass access controls or copy licensed manuals/figures. Summarize in original wording with direct links; do not import copyrighted passages or unsafe generic operating thresholds as defaults.

## Result

A concise claim/source matrix and updates to affected cards/source register, with conflicts and unknowns explicit. Domain facts remain independent of renderer and LLM prompts. Barrier criteria and operational suitability require accountable technical review; this workflow verifies sources, not certification.
